<rule id="7X" priority="CRITICAL" name="DBCLM KYC LEVEL, LOCAL ADDENDA AND OWNERSHIP METHODOLOGY GOVERNANCE">
Ownership methodology must be determined using BOTH:
1. DBCLM KYC Level
2. Adoption Location and applicable Local Addenda requirements
Ownership methodology cannot be selected until both evaluations are completed.



STEP 1 - IDENTIFY ADOPTION LOCATION
Before ownership extraction, identify all Adoption Locations and review all applicable Local Addenda requirements.
Where multiple Adoption Locations exist, apply the strictest ownership requirement unless the Local Addendum explicitly states otherwise.
Applicable Local Addenda requirements include, but are not limited to:
- ownership thresholds,
- inclusive thresholds,
- mandatory drill-down,
- exemption restrictions,
- notional UBO requirements,
- ownership reporting requirements,
- special entity-type requirements.
Local Addenda must always be applied before:
- ownership qualification,
- IBO determination,
- UBO determination,
- stop-rule application,
- drill-down decisions,
- ownership classification.


Where a Local Addendum imposes a stricter ownership requirement than Global KOS, the Local Addendum prevails.
STEP 2 - DETERMINE OWNERSHIP APPROACH
Where DBCLM KYC Level = Global
→ ownershipApproach = GLOBAL
Where DBCLM KYC Level = Streamline
→ ownershipApproach = STREAMLINED

Where DBCLM KYC Level Missing, Blank, Null or Unavailable
→ ownershipApproach = GLOBAL

Never assume STREAMLINED methodology unless DBCLM explicitly identifies KYC Level as Streamline.

GLOBAL OWNERSHIP METHODOLOGY
Where DBCLM KYC Level = Global:
Apply:
- Global Ownership Methodology,
- Applicable Local Addenda requirements,
- Dilution Methodology,
- Domination Assessment,
- Voting Rights Assessment,
- Control Assessment.

Global methodology is NOT dilution-only.
Ownership qualification must consider:
- dilution ownership assessment,
- domination ownership assessment,
- voting-rights assessment,
- control assessment,
- Local ownership overlays.

Where ownership exceeds 50%: Domination Assessment under Rule 10.2 remains mandatory for every ownership relationship.
→ dominationIndicator = YES
→ DominationBasis = D1 Majority Share Ownership
Where voting rights exceed 50%:
→ dominationIndicator = YES
→ DominationBasis = D2 Majority Voting Rights
Where control by other means exists:
→ dominationIndicator = YES
→ DominationBasis = D3 Control Basis

A party may be a dominating owner even where its final diluted ownership interest falls below an IBO or UBO threshold.


STREAMLINED OWNERSHIP METHODOLOGY
Where DBCLM KYC Level = Streamline:

Layer 1 Owners


- retain their directly evidenced ownership position,
- QualifyingInterest = actualOwnershipPercentage,
- apply Global or Local ownership thresholds against the direct ownership position,
- do not apply domination inheritance.

Layer 2 And Above


- apply Domination Inheritance Methodology only,
- do not apply dilution methodology,
- do not multiply ownership percentages across ownership layers,
- do not calculate cumulative indirect ownership percentages.

Where an owner dominates an immediately subordinate entity through:
- ownership greater than 50%,
- voting rights greater than 50%,
- control by other means,
Example:
the dominating owner inherits the full ownership position of the immediately subordinate entity.

ABC Ltd owns 30% of Client.
JKL Ltd owns 60% of ABC Ltd.
Result:

ABC Ltd QualifyingInterest = 30%.
JKL Ltd inherits ABC Ltd's full 30% ownership position.
JKL Ltd QualifyingInterest = 30%.
The result must never be diluted to 18%.





<rule id="9.2.3" name="FORM ADV OWNERSHIP CODE CONVERSION RULE">


This rule applies whenever ownership percentages are sourced from SEC Form ADV ownership disclosures.
Where Form ADV ownership codes are provided instead of exact ownership percentages, apply:
- NA = 4.99%
- A = 6.00%
- B = 11.00%
- C = 25.01%
- D = 50.01%
- E = 75.01%

Special Allocation Rules
- Single E holder = 75.01%
- E + C holders = E 75.00%, C 25.00%
- Single D holder = 74.99%
- D + C holders = D 74.99%, C 25.00%
- Two D holders = 50.00% each
- Single C holder = 49.99%
- Four C holders = 25.00% each
- C with B/A/NA holder(s) = C remains 25.01%; Lower-code holders retain their standard values.

ADV ownership-code percentages are deemed ownership evidence and must be used for:
- ownership extraction,
- ownership qualification,
- IBO determination,
- UBO determination,
- domination assessment,
- ownership inheritance.

Do not raise:
- MISSING_PERCENTAGE,
- SOURCE_DATA_NOT_AVAILABLE,
- ownership percentage advisory requests,
solely because an exact ownership percentage is not disclosed.

Where a more precise ownership percentage is available in another admissible ownership document, the exact percentage prevails.

Where ADV codes are used, governanceBasis must identify the ownership code relied upon.


<rule id="9.0A" priority="CRITICAL" name="COLLECTIVE OWNER, PASSIVE INVESTOR AND LEGEND RESOLUTION RULE">


Before creating any ownership relationship, the agent must determine whether an ownership node represents:
A. A legal entity or natural person
OR
B. A collective ownership label representing multiple parties.



COLLECTIVE LABEL IDENTIFICATION


The following examples must not automatically be treated as legal entities:
- Passive Investors
- Other Shareholders
- Other Owners
- Investor Group
- Group Of Investors
- Institutional Investors
- External Investors
- Co-Investors
- Various Funds
- Group Of Funds
- Other Funds
- Limited Partners
- Other LPs
- Remaining Shareholders
- Minority Investors
- Passive Investor Group
- Investor Consortium



These labels are ownership descriptors and may represent multiple entities, funds, partnerships, investors or ownership interests.
The agent must not automatically classify these labels as a single ownership-bearing legal entity.


MANDATORY RESOLUTION REQUIREMENT



Where a collective ownership label is identified, the agent must review all available sources for clarification, including:
- organisation charts,
- chart footnotes,
- chart legends,
- symbols,
- ownership notes,
- ownership tables,
- explanatory text,
- client-provided emails,
- maker comments,
- checker comments,
- supporting ownership documents.

If clarification identifies the underlying owners, funds, partnerships or entities:
- extract the underlying ownership parties,
- replace the collective label with the resolved ownership parties,
- preserve all supporting evidence.

If clarification cannot be obtained:
- preserve the collective ownership label,
- classify roleCapacity = Information Only,
- do not infer underlying ownership parties,
- raise QA Flag: PARTY_IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED.



LEGEND RESOLUTION RULE



Where any legend, symbol, notation, abbreviation or ownership marker appears, including but not limited to: *, **,#, ^, †, ‡, [1], (A),
the agent must determine whether a legend explanation exists.

The agent must review:
- chart legends,
- footnotes,
- ownership notes,
- document explanations,
- client-provided emails,
- supporting ownership documents.

Where a legend explanation exists:
- apply the explanation to all affected ownership relationships,
- record the interpretation in governanceBasis,
- use the interpreted relationship in ownership extraction.


The agent must never ignore a legend merely because the explanation appears outside the organisation chart.


LEGEND PRECEDENCE

Legend explanations obtained from:
- chart legends,
- chart footnotes,
- ownership notes,
- client-provided clarification emails,
are ownership evidence and must be used when interpreting ownership relationships.


EXAMPLE

Ownership Chart:
ABC Holdings -> 100% Passive Investors *

Legend:
* Passive Investors = Collective ownership consisting of five underlying LP funds.

Client Email:
Passive Investors represent:
Fund A 10%
Fund B 8%
Fund C 6%
Fund D 4%
Fund E 2%

Required Outcome:

Extract:
Fund A
Fund B
Fund C
Fund D
Fund E


Do not extract a single owner called "Passive Investors".


If underlying parties cannot be evidenced, retain "Passive Investors" as Information Only and raise PARTY_IDENTITY_UNRESOLVED.

PROHIBITION


The agent must never:

- assume that a collective ownership label is a single legal entity,
- create ownership links based solely on collective labels,
- ignore ownership legends,
- ignore clarification contained in client emails,
- infer underlying parties where documentary support is not available.


MANDATORY QA FAILURE CONDITIONS


The agent must raise a QA Failure whenever a collective ownership label, passive investor group, shareholder grouping, legend, symbol, notation, or ownership reference cannot be fully resolved using the available documentation.

Examples include:

- Passive Investors identified but underlying investors cannot be determined.
- Other Shareholders identified but underlying shareholders cannot be determined.
- Group of Funds identified but underlying funds cannot be determined.
- Limited Partners identified but underlying LPs cannot be determined.
- Investor Consortium identified but underlying members cannot be determined.
- Ownership legend exists but interpretation cannot be located.
- Symbol, notation or relationship marker exists but no explanation is available.
- Client documentation references a group owner but supporting ownership details are not provided.
- Organisation chart references a collective ownership block but underlying ownership composition is unavailable.

Where any of the above conditions occur:

ownershipReviewStatus = NEED_REVIEW

QAFlag = COLLECTIVE_OWNER_UNRESOLVED
or
QAFlag = LEGEND_INTERPRETATION_MISSING

as applicable.





OWNERSHIP COMPLETENESS REQUIREMENT

The agent must not assume:

- the identity of underlying investors,
- the identity of underlying funds,
- the identity of underlying partnerships,
- ownership percentages,
- ownership distribution,

where documentary evidence is unavailable.


Any unresolved collective ownership representation must be treated as an Ownership Completeness Failure and explicitly disclosed within:
- governanceBasis,
- QA Flags,
- ownershipReviewStatus,
- final ownership summary.


The agent must never silently ignore unresolved collective ownership groups, legends, symbols, or ownership references.
