R1. RECORD DIRECTION CONTRACT (schema semantics — get this right before anything else).
Each record describes ONE party and ONE link. `nameAsSource` is that party — the OWNER. `linkedName` is the entity that party OWNS, one layer CLOSER to the client. Read every record as "nameAsSource is a [relationshipType] of linkedName".
  - `layer`: 0 = the client, 1 = a direct owner of the client, 2 = an owner of a layer-1 entity, and so on. It follows that a record's `layer` equals its `linkedName` record's `layer` + 1.
  - `linkedName` is null ONLY on the layer-0 client record. An evidenced ultimate parent is NOT null here — it names the entity below it and carries `isUltimateParent` = true.
  - A node owned by N owners yields N records sharing ONE `linkedName` with N different `nameAsSource` values. One party owning M entities yields M records sharing ONE `nameAsSource` with M different `linkedName` values.
  - `dilutionOwnershipPercentage` multiplies `actualOwnershipPercentage` along the `linkedName` chain DOWN to the client; the §9.3 sum check groups records BY `linkedName` to total that target's shareholders.
Do NOT score a record as direction-inverted merely because it points at an entity it owns — that IS the correct direction. An inverted record is one whose `linkedName` names the subject's OWNER.

R2. roleCapacity PRECEDENCE LADDER (§12.0) — walk from rung 1 downward, FIRST MATCH WINS. Never weigh two rungs against each other and never skip a matching rung in favour of a lower one. Type invariants gate the ladder: a Natural Person is NEVER assigned IBO, a Non-Natural Person is NEVER assigned UBO, and a rung whose type invariant fails is skipped. These 22 values are also the complete `roleCapacity` enum.
   1 Client — the anchored client at layer 0.
   2 Unknown / Needs Review — ownership direction or party identity unresolved. Fail closed BEFORE classifying.
   3 Information Only — attribution only, no independent ownership support (an ORBIS GUO / GUP / ultimate-parent label).
   4 Listed Parent — an evidenced, permitted listed-entity endpoint applies at this node. Two cases, and they differ: where the listed entity IS the client, §11.2's stop rule halts UBO/IBO determination entirely, so `isIBO` / `isUBO` false across the set is CORRECT and must not be scored as a ladder violation; a MID-CHAIN listed parent takes this label via §11.1 only and does NOT inherit that halt.
   5 Regulated Parent — an evidenced, permitted regulated-entity exemption applies.
   6 Government Owner — evidenced government / ministry / state-body ownership.
   7 Exempt Entity — any other evidenced, permitted exemption, e.g. a majority-owned subsidiary of a listed entity.
   8 UBO — Natural Person meeting the applicable ownership threshold.
   9 IBO — Non-Natural Person over the applicable IBO threshold.
  10 Control-Based IBO/UBO — qualifies on evidenced control or domination rather than on the ownership threshold.
  11 Notional UBO — fires ONLY where an applicable Local Addendum expressly requires the affirmative classification.
  12 Possible Notional UBO — the DEFAULT where notional-UBO / CSM fallback is required but no such local override applies.
  13 General Partner  14 Limited Partner  15 Trustee  16 Settlor  17 Beneficiary  18 Protector  19 Foundation Controller — each an evidenced role qualifying for no rung above.
  20 Direct Owner — a layer-1 owner of the client qualifying for no rung above. A layer-1 THRESHOLD FAILURE lands here.
  21 Indirect Owner — an owner at layer 2 or deeper qualifying for no rung above. A layer-2+ threshold failure lands here.
  22 Non-Qualifying Owner — the link is not beneficially ownership-bearing: an evidenced nominee, custodial, escrow or agent arrangement with no beneficial interest. NEVER the outcome of a threshold failure.
The ladder assigns only the LABEL. It never alters `isIBO` / `isUBO`, which are set independently from thresholds and evidence — a stop-rule rung (4, 5, 6, 7) is not licence to zero those booleans.

  <criterion name="Ownership Direction">
  Per section 9.1 and the ORGCHART rule, applying the R1 direction contract. Direction must never be inferred from visual placement, arrow orientation, SmartArt layout, OCR extraction order, PDF rendering sequence or chart proximity; it must rest on documentary evidence — shareholder tables, ownership percentages, registry wording, ownership statements, chart legends, explanatory notes.

Do NOT score a record as inverted merely because it points at an entity it owns; that is the correct direction, and calling it an inversion is itself a false finding.
  Natural-person rule: where a Natural Person and a Non-Natural Person are linked, the person is the OWNER unless the documents explicitly evidence the reverse. So a record whose `nameAsSource` is a Natural Person should normally carry a Non-Natural Person in `linkedName`; a Natural Person appearing as another record's `linkedName` asserts that a person is OWNED by an entity and needs explicit evidence. A Natural Person recorded as a subsidiary, controlled entity or ownership endpoint of a corporate entity, unevidenced, is IMPORTANT.
  ORGCHART fail-closed: reading a chart upward is a HYPOTHESIS, never evidence, and never by itself supports an emitted link. Where the documents leave direction unconfirmed the extractor must NOT emit a presumed edge — it must flag `OWNERSHIP_DIRECTION_REVIEW_REQUIRED` and leave the direction unresolved, the record taking ladder rung 2 "Unknown / Needs Review" (R2) rather than any classified rung. An unresolved-direction record that carries a classification instead of the flag plus rung 2 is IMPORTANT.

  <criterion name="IBO / UBO Classification Accuracy">
  Per rule 12 (Global IBO / UBO definitions and thresholds): an IBO is a Non-Natural Person that directly or indirectly owns the client above the IBO threshold; a UBO is a Natural Person who owns OR controls the client above the UBO threshold. This IS schema-backed via `isIBO`, `isUBO`, and `roleCapacity`. Apply the GLOBAL thresholds by `dueDiligenceLevel` — IBO: &gt; 25% (CDD and EDD); UBO: &gt; 25% (CDD), &gt; 10% (EDD) — UNLESS a stricter/inclusive Local Addendum threshold applies (rules 13 / 13.6), in which case the local threshold governs. Under the STREAMLINED pathway thresholds are instead tested against the DEEMED INHERITED position produced by the Domination Methodology (section 8.3) — a &gt; 50% holder inherits the dominated entity's full client position (so a 55% holder of an entity holding 70% of the client is a UBO at a deemed 70%, not 38.5%), and a holder of 50% or less inherits nothing. On the GLOBAL pathway thresholds are tested against CUMULATIVE (diluted) ownership — the product of the direct percentages along the owner's path to the client (rule 10.1 / 12.3) — NOT against a single direct link's `actualOwnershipPercentage`; where the chain is fully evidenced, verify the diluted computation and that the resulting `isIBO` / `isUBO` matches (e.g. a link at 80% direct whose cumulative interest is 24% is not a CDD UBO). Verify `isIBO` / `isUBO` / `roleCapacity` are consistent with the applicable threshold and the ownership/control evidence: no qualifying owner left unflagged, no sub-threshold owner over-classified — EXCEPT where §12.4's CONTROL-BASED IBO QUALIFICATION applies, which is NOT over-classification: a Non-Natural Person evidencing one of rule 10.2's control or domination bases (the closed list at R3) is correctly `isIBO` = true at ladder rung 10 even below the ownership threshold, and scoring that as over-classification is itself a false finding. Control-based qualification (rule 10 domination) is counted below the ownership threshold for BOTH party types — UBO for a Natural Person via §12.2.1 criteria 3/4/5, IBO for a Non-Natural Person via §12.4 — and in either case the specific basis relied upon must be named in `governanceBasis`; an `isIBO`/`isUBO` set on control with no basis named is IMPORTANT. Separately, a STREAMLINED record carrying `NO_INHERITED_POSITION` with `dilutionOwnershipPercentage` = 0 is the defined §8.3.3 outcome for a non-dominating owner at layer 2+, not a missing figure: scoring it as a gap, or expecting MISSING_PERCENTAGE there, is a false finding, and the type invariant respected — a Natural Person is never `isIBO`, a Non-Natural Person is never `isUBO`. Apply the rule 12.2.1 UBO-qualification gate: a Natural Person with `isUBO` = true must satisfy at least ONE qualification criterion — Global threshold, stricter Local threshold, control, domination, voting-rights, or diluted-ownership (cumulative) qualification. A Natural Person flagged `isUBO` = true who meets none of these (e.g. an 8% direct owner with no control/domination/voting/diluted basis) is over-classified — the correct output is `isUBO` = false with a non-UBO `roleCapacity` ("Direct Owner" / "Non-Qualifying Owner") — and is IMPORTANT. A wrong `isIBO` / `isUBO` / `roleCapacity` is IMPORTANT; a qualifying owner dropped from `extracted_records` entirely is CRITICAL under Entity Completeness.
  Section 12.0: roleCapacity is assigned by the precedence ladder reproduced at R2, first match wins. Walk R2 yourself for each record and verify no record took a lower rung while a higher rung's condition was satisfied — for example an evidenced listed parent recorded as "IBO" (rung 9) when rung 4 applied, or an unresolved-direction record classified at all instead of taking rung 2. A ladder violation is IMPORTANT. Verify rung 22 "Non-Qualifying Owner" was used only for links that are not beneficially ownership-bearing: an owner that merely fell below a threshold belongs on rung 20 or 21. Verify the threshold test used qualifyingInterest — the cumulative diluted product on GLOBAL, the deemed inherited position on STREAMLINED — and never a single direct link's actualOwnershipPercentage; testing the direct figure is CRITICAL. LAYER-1 EXCEPTION: this prohibition applies to owners at layer 2 or deeper. For a LAYER-1 owner, qualifyingInterest EQUALS the direct percentage (§12.3 / §12.4 — no intermediate layers exist to multiply through), so testing the direct actualOwnershipPercentage against the threshold on a layer-1 record is the correct, required test, not a violation; do not flag it as testing "the direct figure."
  </criterion>

  <criterion name="Domination & Control Indicators">
  Per section 10: the control / domination indicators listed at R3 — the closed list — must be captured where explicitly evidenced, without concluding domination absent explicit support; ownership/voting mismatches and control-based UBO triggers must be flagged. This IS schema-backed via `dominationOwnership` / `dominationVotingRights` (numeric percentages) and the per-record `dominationIndicator` (YES/NO), `controlRights` (free text), `ownershipVotingMismatch`, and `controlBasedUBOAssessmentRequired` booleans. A `dominationIndicator` of YES asserted without documented control evidence, or an `ownershipVotingMismatch` / `controlBasedUBOAssessmentRequired` value that contradicts the cited evidence, is IMPORTANT. Diluted figures are recorded per record: `dilutionOwnershipPercentage` must equal the product of `actualOwnershipPercentage` along the record's path to the client (§10.1/§12.3), and `dilutionVotingRightsPercentage` the same product over `actualVotingRightsPercentage` (§10.1.1) — but 0 WITH a `DILUTED_VOTING_INCOMPLETE` qaFlag whenever any link on the path lacks a stated voting %, never the ownership product and never a bare 0 without the flag (the flag marks unknown vs a genuine 0, per OC.3). §10.1.1 forbids assuming voting = ownership or substituting the ownership figure for a missing voting figure; a `dilutionVotingRightsPercentage` set to the ownership product (or any nonzero value) across a path with a missing-voting link is a substitution violation. Diluted voting is not tested against the ownership-based IBO/UBO thresholds, but informs the control-based UBO / domination assessment (§10.2). Where the chain is fully evidenced, verify both; a diluted-voting figure set to the ownership product despite a missing-voting link, a missing-link 0 lacking the DILUTED_VOTING_INCOMPLETE flag, or a mis-multiplied `dilutionOwnershipPercentage`, is IMPORTANT.   </criterion>

  <criterion name="Conflict Preservation">
  Per section 14: where documents conflict on ownership percentage, voting rights, shareholder list, or entity type, all versions must be preserved and the conflict must not be silently resolved or one version silently dropped. Where the source documents show a percentage or shareholder-list conflict but the record set is silent about it (no `conflictTag`, or a `conflictTag` of "C: clear" against clearly conflicting sources), that is a dropped-conflict failure — IMPORTANT.

  <criterion name="Structural Validation">

  Enums: `relationshipType` must come from the R4 list, must be null only on the client record, and must not contradict `itemType` or the cited evidence; `roleCapacity` must be one of the 22 R2 values.   Container shapes (R8): verify top-level `clientAnchor`, `outOfBounds`, `qaFlags` and `advisory` are JSON OBJECTS — including when empty — and that the per-record `outOfBounds` is an object while the per-record `qaFlags` is an array.   Reasoning format (R5): `governanceBasis` must carry all eight labelled parts, in order, on one line each, with no text outside them; within part [1] the trace tokens must precede Classification and Conclusion, and part [1] Classification must equal part [7] FinalOwnershipClassification verbatim.   Canonical record order: extracted_records must be sorted by layer ascending (a record with no integer layer sorts ahead of layer 0), then linkedName (null/empty first), then nameAsSource, then relationshipType, then documentName, then pageNumber, with ids assigned 1..N over the sorted list — verify ids are sequential and the order holds. Where two records tie on every key, the rule breaks the tie on the canonical JSON serialization with id and the free-text narrative fields excluded; a tie you cannot resolve is not a finding.
