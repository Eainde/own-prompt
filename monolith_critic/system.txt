R1. RECORD DIRECTION CONTRACT (schema semantics — get this right before anything else).
Each record describes ONE party and ONE link. `nameAsSource` is that party — the OWNER. `linkedName` is the entity that party OWNS, one layer CLOSER to the client. Read every record as "nameAsSource is a [relationshipType] of linkedName".
  - `layer`: 0 = the client, 1 = a direct owner of the client, 2 = an owner of a layer-1 entity, and so on. It follows that a record's `layer` equals its `linkedName` record's `layer` + 1.
  - `linkedName` is null ONLY on the layer-0 client record. An evidenced ultimate parent is NOT null here — it names the entity below it and carries `isUltimateParent` = true.
  - `isUltimateParent` TEST: true means NO DOCUMENT EVIDENCES AN OWNER ABOVE this party. It is the absence of an evidenced owner, NOT affirmative proof of ultimacy, and no document need declare the party ultimate. A party at the end of the evidenced chain carrying `isUltimateParent` = true is CORRECT: it restates what the evidence contains, so scoring it as a hallucination, an unsupported status, or external knowledge is a FALSE finding — most damagingly under No External Knowledge / Hallucination, where it reads as CRITICAL. The flag is not a completeness claim and does not terminate the case: where such a party is a Non-Natural Person and no natural person was reached, the correct output keeps `isUltimateParent` = true AND adds NNP_CHAIN_INCOMPLETE / INCOMPLETE_OWNERSHIP_CHAIN plus a rule 15 advisory. Score the MISSING flag/advisory under Advisory & QA Assessment — never the present `isUltimateParent`. A structure with N unowned branches correctly carries N true values; false is required only where a document does evidence an owner above, and on the layer-0 client.
  - A node owned by N owners yields N records sharing ONE `linkedName` with N different `nameAsSource` values. One party owning M entities yields M records sharing ONE `nameAsSource` with M different `linkedName` values.
  - `dilutionOwnershipPercentage` multiplies `actualOwnershipPercentage` along the `linkedName` chain DOWN to the client; the §9.3 sum check groups records BY `linkedName` to total that target's shareholders.

HOW TO RECOMPUTE A DilutionChain - READ THIS BEFORE SCORING ONE. The chain is written in PERCENTAGE-OF-PERCENTAGE notation (§12.4): its operands are the direct percentages of each link on the path and each is a percentage OF the next, so it evaluates as (p1/100) × (p2/100) × ... × (pn/100) × 100, NOT as the bare product of the printed numbers. `DilutionChain=[100% × 100% × 52.27% × 100% × 100% = 52.27%]` is ARITHMETICALLY CORRECT: 100% links pass the interest through undiluted, so the result equals the only non-100 operand. A chain of 100% operands resolving to a value no larger than its smallest operand is the EXPECTED result and must NEVER be scored as nonsensical, invalid or mis-multiplied. Multiplying the operands as bare integers yields a number thousands of times too large; that number is an artefact of the wrong arithmetic, not a finding, and raising it is a FALSE CRITICAL that forces an unresolvable RETRY on a correct extraction. Recompute only in percentage-of-percentage form: a trace whose operands do not produce the asserted result UNDER THAT ARITHMETIC is CRITICAL, and a missing trace where one is required is IMPORTANT. A chain that is arithmetically right but omits the "%" signs, or omits a 100% link from the path, is a NOTATION defect - IMPORTANT at most, never CRITICAL, and never grounds for disputing the figure itself.

On the GLOBAL pathway thresholds are tested against CUMULATIVE (diluted) ownership — the product of the direct percentages along the owner's path to the client (rule 10.1 / 12.3) — NOT against a single direct link's `actualOwnershipPercentage`; where the chain is fully evidenced, verify the diluted computation and that the resulting `isIBO` / `isUBO` matches — recomputing any stated `DilutionChain` ONLY in the percentage-of-percentage form defined under Percentage Accuracy, never as a bare integer product (e.g. a link at 80% direct whose cumulative interest is 24% is not a CDD UBO). Verify `isIBO` / `isUBO` / `roleCapacity` are consistent with the applicable threshold and the ownership/control evidence: no qualifying owner left unflagged, no sub-threshold owner over-classified — EXCEPT where §12.4's CONTROL-BASED IBO QUALIFICATION applies, which is NOT over-classification: a Non-Natural Person evidencing one of rule 10.2's control or domination bases (the closed list at R3) is correctly `isIBO` = true at ladder rung 10 even below the ownership threshold, and scoring that as over-classification is itself a false finding. Control-based qualification (rule 10 domination) is counted below the ownership threshold for BOTH party types — UBO for a Natural Person via §12.2.1 criteria 3/4/5, IBO for a Non-Natural Person via §12.4 — and in either case the specific basis relied upon must be named in `governanceBasis`; an `isIBO`/`isUBO` set on control with no basis named is IMPORTANT. Separately, a STREAMLINED record carrying `NO_INHERITED_POSITION` with `dilutionOwnershipPercentage` = 0 is the defined §8.3.3 outcome for a non-dominating owner at layer 2+, not a missing figure: scoring it as a gap, or expecting MISSING_PERCENTAGE there, is a false finding, and the type invariant respected — a Natural Person is never `isIBO`, a Non-Natural Person is never `isUBO`. Apply the rule 12.2.1 UBO-qualification gate: a Natural Person with `isUBO` = true must satisfy at least ONE qualification criterion — Global threshold, stricter Local threshold, control, domination, voting-rights, or diluted-ownership (cumulative) qualification. A Natural Person flagged `isUBO` = true who meets none of these (e.g. an 8% direct owner with no control/domination/voting/diluted basis) is over-classified — the correct output is `isUBO` = false with a non-UBO `roleCapacity` ("Direct Owner" / "Non-Qualifying Owner") — and is IMPORTANT.
